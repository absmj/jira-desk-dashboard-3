# 16x2 HD44780 LCD (branch `feature/lcd1602-display`)

Replaces the Nokia 5110 / ILI9341 with a 16 x 2 character LCD. Everything else (data files, pager, alerts, BLE receiver,
audio) is shared. Build flag: `-DDISPLAY_HD44780`. Envs: `sim-lcd` (Wokwi) and `supermini-lcd` (hardware).

**Assumption:** the module has the usual **PCF8574 I2C backpack** (address `0x27`; `0x3F` for the PCF8574A variant:
add `-DLCD_I2C_ADDR=0x3F`). A bare 16-pin module needs 6 GPIOs and an extra 4-bit port class; say so if that is what you bought.

Run the PC tests: `ARDUINOJSON_SRC=<ArduinoJson>/src tools/native_tests/run.sh` (the 16x2 block is the last one).

## Wiring

| Backpack | SuperMini | Note |
|---|---|---|
| SDA | GPIO8 | `pins::kI2cSda`; shared with the DS3231 later (it is 0x68) |
| SCL | GPIO9 | `pins::kI2cScl` |
| GND | GND | |
| VCC | **5 V** | most 1602 modules need 5 V for readable contrast |

The five Nokia pins (GPIO0-3, 10) are free again. That helps the pin shortage found for the enclosure buttons (VOL+/VOL-):
see `docs/battery-plan.md`; the DFPlayer BUSY / TX lines no longer have to be sacrificed.

**5 V warning:** a backpack powered from 5 V usually has 4.7-10 kohm pull-ups to **5 V** on SDA and SCL. The ESP32-C3 GPIOs are
not 5 V tolerant. Either cut/remove those two pull-up resistors and use the 3.3 V pull-ups of the DS3231 module (or add your own to 3.3 V),
or put a level shifter on SDA/SCL, or power the module from 3.3 V (many 1602s are then too faint, but 3.3 V-rated ones exist).
Check the real board before connecting it.

Longer text is cut with a trailing `.`. An alert message longer than 2 lines is shown two lines at a time (2.5 s each).

## Azerbaijani letters

The HD44780 ROM has none of ə ı İ ö ü ç ş ğ (and capitals), but 8 programmable characters. Per screen the driver gives each distinct
special letter on screen a CGRAM slot (bitmaps come from `AzText`, the same 5x8 shapes as before) and keeps them between screens.
If one screen needs **more than 8 different special letters**, the extra ones are shown as plain ASCII (ə -> e, ş -> s).
Two-line, 32-cell screens in Azerbaijani rarely get there; the tests count such frames. The ROM's `\` and `~` are replaced with `/` and `-`.
Other characters show as `?`. Glyph quality: 5x8 dots are tight, and the shapes were drawn for a pixel panel, so judge them on the real glass.

## Power and battery

The 16x2 module draws about 1-2 mA with the backlight off and about 15-25 mA with it on (typical, unmeasured), plus 5 V generation from a LiPo
(boost converter with its own quiet current). Keep the backlight off by default (`ITextDisplay::setBacklight`); a button could turn it on for
a few seconds. Revisit `docs/battery-plan.md`: its display line (Nokia 0.2-0.3 mA) no longer applies.

## Enclosure

The square 78 mm case cannot hold the 80 x 36 mm module, so this branch has a wider one: **98 x 56 x 43.5 mm** (with lid).
`enclosure/make_enclosure_lcd.py` writes `enclosure/stl_lcd/`, `enclosure/check_fit_lcd.py` checks the placeholder components for collisions
(passes with the typical sizes listed in the script, not with measured ones), `enclosure/viewer_lcd.html` is the 3D preview.
It is thicker than the square one because the module with its I2C backpack is about 18 mm deep and the boards and battery sit behind it.
Buttons POWER / VOL- / VOL+ are on the top edge, above the module. The two label pockets are 62 x 8 mm (logo) and 66 x 7.4 mm (team name).
Module size, mounting holes (75 x 31 mm), bezel height (7.9 mm above the PCB) and backpack position are typical values: measure your module.
